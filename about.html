Our story

Started with one idea: insurance should feel like a relationship, not a transaction.

Uddin Insurance was founded to give drivers, homeowners, and small business owners in the DC metro area an alternative to call-center insurance — an independent agency that actually shops the market on your behalf and stays reachable long after the policy is signed.

Today we work across auto, home, and commercial lines, comparing two dozen carriers to build coverage around each client's real situation.

What we stand for

Three things that don't change.

Independent

We're not tied to one carrier, so our recommendation is always about your situation — not a sales quota.

Responsive

Calls and messages get answered by people who know your policy, not a queue.

Local

Based in the DC metro area and licensed across MD, DC, and VA — we understand the region we serve.
