Common questions

Auto Insurance FAQ

How fast can I get proof of insurance?
Once your policy is bound, we can send digital ID cards the same day — often within minutes of your call.
Will you re-shop my policy at renewal?
Yes. We re-quote your policy across our carrier network before every renewal so you're not just automatically re-billed at a higher rate.
Can you insure a driver with a recent accident or violation?
In most cases, yes. Because we work with multiple carriers, we can usually find coverage even when a single insurer would decline or non-renew you.
Do you write policies outside Maryland?
We're licensed across Maryland, DC, and Virginia, with additional state licensing available — ask your advisor.
