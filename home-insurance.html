Common questions

Home Insurance FAQ

Does home insurance cover flooding?
Standard policies typically exclude flood damage. We can pair your policy with separate flood coverage where it makes sense for your property.
How is my dwelling coverage amount determined?
It's based on the estimated cost to rebuild your home at current materials and labor costs — not its market value.
I rent out a property — can my tenant's renters policy replace mine?
No. Your tenant's renters policy only covers their belongings and liability. You still need a landlord policy to cover the structure and your liability as the owner.
