Common questions

Commercial Insurance FAQ

Do I need workers' comp if I only have one or two employees?
Most states require it once you have any employees, with some exceptions for very small or family-run businesses. We'll confirm what applies to your state and structure.
What's the difference between a BOP and buying policies separately?
A Business Owners Policy bundles general liability and commercial property into one package, usually at a lower combined premium than purchasing each separately.
Can you review our coverage as we grow?
Yes — we recommend an annual coverage review, and we're also available any time your operations, headcount, or equipment change materially.
